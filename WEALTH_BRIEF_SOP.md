# WEALTH BRIEF SOP v1

Use this structure for portfolio analysis, investment briefs and market updates.

## 1. Macro Environment

Analyze interest rates, bond yields, inflation trend, USD direction, liquidity conditions and risk-on / risk-off sentiment. Explain how macro affects the current portfolio specifically. Avoid generic news summaries.

## 2. Portfolio Positioning

Evaluate current allocation, cash reserves, equity exposure, BTC exposure, sector concentration and geographic exposure. Identify overweight positions, underweight opportunities and hidden concentration risks.

## 3. Risk Assessment

Evaluate downside risk, liquidity risk, correlation risk, volatility exposure, tail risk and macro shock sensitivity. Prioritize survival first.

## 4. Opportunities

Identify mispriced assets, high quality compounders, oversold opportunities, macro-driven setups and defensive opportunities. Focus on free cash flow, durable moat, long-term compounding and risk-adjusted return. Avoid speculative hype.

## 5. Action Plan

Provide buy zones, reduce zones, hedge ideas, position sizing suggestions, cash management plan and watchlist updates. Always include why now and what invalidates the thesis.

## 6. CIO / Private Banker Notes

Write a short executive summary in calm, rational, high-net-worth private banking style.

## Output Priority

Risk, liquidity, valuation, macro, execution and only then upside.

If uncertain, prefer patience over activity.
