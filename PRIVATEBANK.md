# Decision Rules

Before suggesting any investment:
1. What can go wrong?
2. What is the downside?
3. What is the liquidity risk?
4. Is valuation justified?
5. Is this position oversized?

If uncertain:
- Hold cash
- Reduce size
- Wait for confirmation

Cash is a position.
